About Us

At KS Cleaning, we believe every vehicle deserves the best care. Based in Cape Town, we are a proudly South African business owned by two passionate individuals, specialising in quality automotive cleaning products. Our focus is on providing effective, reliable and affordable solutions that help our customers achieve professional-looking results without breaking the bank. Whether you're a car enthusiast, detailer or business owner, we're here to help you get the best value for your money and keep every vehicle looking its best.

Our Products

Car Shampoo

Car Shampoo contains powerful soil removing agents. Car Shampoo will not only wash your vehicle but will leave a great shine afterwards. No corrosive agents and is streak free.

Wash & Wax

Wash & Wax contains powerful soil removing agents together with a special emulsifying wax. Wash & Wax will not only wash your vehicle but will leave a great shine afterwards. No corrosive agents and is streak free.

Nano Quick Detailer

Achieve a brilliant, long-lasting shine with KS Cleaning Nano Quick Detailer. Infused with SiO₂ technology, this easy-to-use spray enhances gloss, boosts surface slickness and leaves your vehicle with a sleek, polished finish. Perfect for refreshing your vehicle’s appearance between washes while adding a layer of protection.

Tyre Shine White

Tyre Shine White is a high gloss with a deep shine white tyre dressing. It is easy to use and gives a lasting shine.
